# Paper 2: Nutritional Needs for Exercise in the Heat

# Citation

Burke LM. Nutritional Needs for Exercise in the Heat. 2001.

# Purpose

This paper examines nutritional strategies that can help athletes maintain performance and recover effectively when exercising in hot environments.

## Key Findings

# Hydration

* Athletes should begin exercise well hydrated.
* Fluid intake should occur before, during, and after exercise.
* Rehydration is important following significant sweat losses.
* Electrolyte replacement supports recovery from dehydration.

# Carbohydrate Requirements

* Exercise in hot conditions increases carbohydrate utilization.
* Athletes may require additional carbohydrate intake to support performance and recovery.
* Glycogen stores should be replenished after exercise.

# Sports Drinks

* Sports drinks containing carbohydrates and electrolytes can support both hydration and energy needs.
* Such beverages may encourage greater voluntary fluid intake.

# Electrolyte Replacement

* Electrolytes lost through sweat should be replaced during recovery.
* Electrolyte replacement assists fluid retention and restoration of hydration status.

# Protein

* Current evidence is insufficient to recommend special protein supplementation specifically because exercise occurred in the heat.
