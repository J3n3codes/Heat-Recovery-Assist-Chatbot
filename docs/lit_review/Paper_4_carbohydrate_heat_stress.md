# Paper 4: Heat Stress, Dehydration, and Carbohydrate Utilization During Endurance Exercise

# Citation

Mougin et al. (2025)

# Purpose

This systematic review and meta-analysis examined the effects of heat stress and dehydration on carbohydrate utilization during endurance exercise.

# Key Findings

# Heat Stress and Carbohydrate Use

* Exercising in hot environments increases carbohydrate utilization.
* Heat stress may accelerate depletion of glycogen stores.
* Athletes may fatigue earlier when exercising under heat stress conditions.

# Dehydration

* Dehydration further increases carbohydrate oxidation during exercise.
* Performance may be negatively affected when heat stress and dehydration occur simultaneously.
* Maintaining hydration is important for both performance and recovery.

# Recovery Nutrition

* Recovery should focus on restoring hydration status.
* Recovery nutrition should support replenishment of carbohydrate stores.
* Athletes may benefit from carbohydrate-containing meals or snacks following exercise in the heat.

# Performance Considerations

* Heat stress places additional metabolic demands on athletes.
* Appropriate hydration and nutrition strategies may help reduce performance decline.
