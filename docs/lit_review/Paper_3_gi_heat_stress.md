# Paper 3: Gastrointestinal Perturbations During Exertional Heat Stress

# Citation

Costa et al. (2019)

# Purpose

This paper examines gastrointestinal disturbances that may occur during exercise in hot environments and discusses factors that influence gastrointestinal health and performance.

## Key Findings

# Gastrointestinal Symptoms

Athletes exercising in hot environments may experience:

* Nausea
* Bloating
* Abdominal discomfort
* Flatulence
* Urge to defecate
* Gastrointestinal distress

These symptoms may become more common as heat stress increases.

# Hydration

* Dehydration may worsen gastrointestinal symptoms.
* Excessive fluid intake may also contribute to gastrointestinal discomfort.
* Appropriate fluid replacement is important for recovery and performance.

# Cooling Strategies

Potential approaches discussed include:

* Cold water ingestion
* Cool fluid ingestion
* Internal cooling strategies
* Heat acclimation programs

These strategies may help reduce heat strain during exercise.

# Nutrition Considerations

* Small amounts of carbohydrate intake during exercise may help maintain gastrointestinal function.
* Protein intake during exercise in hot environments may increase gastrointestinal symptoms in some athletes.
* Individual tolerance should be considered.
